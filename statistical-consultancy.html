Statistical Consultancy

Methodological and analytical support for doctoral scholars, faculty, institutions, companies and survey agencies — from research design through to analysis, reporting and publication.

Services

Research Design & Sampling

Study design, sample size determination, sampling plans for surveys and field studies, and selection of the appropriate statistical tests.

Instrument & Scale Validation

Questionnaire design, pilot testing, reliability and validity assessment, exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis.

Structural Equation Modelling

Covariance-based and PLS-SEM, mediation and moderation analysis, multi-group comparison and model fit diagnostics.

Survey & Field Data Analytics

Data cleaning, weighting, descriptive and inferential analysis, regression and categorical models, with clearly written interpretation.

Econometrics & Time Series

ARIMA and GARCH family models, volatility and wavelet analysis, panel data, cointegration and forecasting for financial and economic data.

Impact Evaluation

Quasi-experimental designs, difference-in-differences, propensity score matching and longitudinal analysis for programme evaluation.

Bibliometric & Systematic Reviews

Scopus and Web of Science data retrieval, co-occurrence and co-citation mapping, PRISMA-based systematic review support.

Predictive Analytics & Machine Learning

Classification and prediction models, text analytics and model validation for research and business applications.

Training Workshops & FDPs

Hands-on programmes on research methodology and data analysis for universities, colleges and government institutes.

Software and Tools

Statistical packagesSPSS, AMOS, SmartPLS, EViews, Minitab, STATA, SAS Enterprise
ProgrammingR, Python, Base SAS
Data handling & reportingMS-Excel, Mendeley, LaTeX
Over 100 invited sessions as resource person on research methodology and data analysis at universities, UGC-HRDC and Malaviya Mission teacher training centres, IITs, NITs, state administrative institutes and professional bodies across India.

Who This Is For

  • Doctoral scholars and post-doctoral researchers
  • Faculty preparing papers for indexed journals
  • Universities, colleges and research centres
  • CSR foundations, NGOs and development agencies
  • Companies needing survey and market analytics
  • Government departments and public institutes

Support is provided for study design, analysis and interpretation. Work is carried out in line with research integrity norms: analysis is reported as it is found, and authorship or acknowledgement is agreed in advance.
