Training & Academic Outreach

A record of invited lectures, faculty development programmes and research methodology workshops delivered at universities, teacher training centres and government institutes, together with editorial responsibilities and academic recognition.

Resource Person Record

100+Invited sessions
20+Years as resource person
30+Host institutions
10+Conferences convened or chaired

Sessions have been delivered since 2002 as part of university service. Programmes are arranged by the inviting institution and conducted under its academic calendar.

Themes Taught

Research Methodology

Research design, formulation of hypotheses, sampling criteria and sample size, questionnaire construction and scale development.

Data Analysis Software

Hands-on sessions in SPSS, AMOS, SmartPLS, EViews, R, SAS and advanced MS-Excel for social science and management research.

Statistical Techniques

Descriptive and inferential statistics, correlation and regression, analysis of variance and covariance, and general linear models.

Structural Equation Modelling

Measurement and structural models, mediation and moderation analysis, and model fit assessment using AMOS and SmartPLS.

Econometrics & Time Series

ARIMA and GARCH family models, volatility and wavelet analysis, panel data, cointegration and forecasting for financial and economic data.

Predictive Analytics

Classification and prediction models, text analytics, machine learning for research applications, and model validation.

Bibliometrics & Reviews

Scopus and Web of Science retrieval, co-occurrence and co-citation mapping, and PRISMA-based systematic review method.

Research Writing & Publishing

Data interpretation and reporting, research poster and paper preparation, and publishing in quality indexed journals.

ICT for Academics

Cloud tools for teaching and research, e-learning platforms, digital content for governance training, and reference management.

Statistical packagesSPSS, AMOS, SmartPLS, EViews, Minitab, STATA, SAS Enterprise
ProgrammingR, Python, Base SAS
