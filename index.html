Profile

Prabhat Mittal is a Professor at Satyawati College (Evening), University of Delhi, where he has taught since 2003 and has served as Professor since 2018. He holds a Ph.D. in Supply Chain Management from the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi, an M.Phil. in Operational Research from the Faculty of Mathematical Sciences, and completed a post-doctoral fellowship at the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, University of Minnesota, USA.

His work spans student community engagement and higher education for the Sustainable Development Goals, applied statistics and data analytics, financial econometrics and supply chain analysis. He has directed research and evaluation projects funded by the ICSSR, UGC, HIPA Shimla and the Bureau of Police Research & Development, and has served as a statistical analyst on national surveys for the Ministry of Consumer Affairs and the World Health Organization.

He is an Editor of PLOS ONE and the Journal of Probability and Statistics, and serves on the editorial boards of Scientific Reports (Nature) and other journals. He is a sought-after resource person on research methodology and data analysis, having conducted more than a hundred faculty development programmes and workshops across India.
