Invitation for Research Collaboration

I welcome collaboration with faculty members, research scholars, practitioners and institutions in India and abroad on research articles, edited books, book chapters and funded research projects.

Ways to Collaborate

Joint Research Articles

Co-authored papers for Scopus, SCIE and SSCI indexed journals. I can contribute to study design, quantitative modelling, data analysis and manuscript development, and I particularly welcome cross-country comparative studies.

Edited Books and Book Chapters

Co-editing proposals for Springer, Elsevier, Taylor & Francis, IGI Global and other publishers, and chapter contributions to volumes I edit. I have authored and edited eight books to date.

Collaborative Research Projects

Joint proposals for ICSSR, UGC, ICMR, DST and state agency funding, as well as institutional and international partnerships for survey-based and evaluation studies.

Doctoral and Faculty Support

Co-supervision of doctoral work, methodology mentoring for research scholars, and research methodology sessions for faculty development programmes.

Research Interests for Joint Work

  • Student community engagement and university outreach
  • Higher education policy, quality and the SDGs
  • Service learning, employability and learning analytics
  • Sustainability, CSR and inclusive growth
  • Applied statistics, SEM and predictive analytics
  • Financial econometrics and mathematical finance
  • Supply chain analytics and operational research
  • Bibliometric and systematic review studies
Preference is given to proposals with international co-authorship and collaboration across institutions and geographical regions, and to work that leads to publication in indexed journals or with reputed academic publishers.
